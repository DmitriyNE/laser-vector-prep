# Known defects

Open defects first, then fixed ones. One line each; details go in the log or an issue.

| ID | Status | Area | Description | Found | Fixed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DEF-14 | fixed | geometry | Cancelling during a coverage audit could take seconds (18 s in a debug build): stroking the paths and clustering the loops before the boolean never checked for cancellation, and clustering was quadratic in nested fill rings, since every ring's box overlaps every other's. Clustering now skips boxes inside an already processed one (same clusters, one query per island) and both check for cancellation. Found by the Firefox cancel test on CI after D36 made the fill fast enough to reach the audit before the cancel. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
| DEF-13 | fixed | wasm | In the browser, more threads made evaluation slower: an 8×8 panel took 53 s with ten threads and 10 s with two, because std's wasm allocator serialises every allocation on a spin lock that starves under contention. The wasm build now has its own allocator (D36): 3.3 s with ten threads. Found by timing D35 in Chrome. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
| DEF-12 | fixed | geometry | Near-coincident edges and arcs whose vertices did not line up could leave a boolean's boundary chain open: that part of the composite was dropped with a warning (D30). Vertex snapping also took the first vertex in range instead of the nearest, which could leave a sliver. Now resolved exactly where possible, else by a robust polygon boolean (D34). Found by fuzzing. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
| DEF-11 | fixed | geometry | A PDF whose transforms produced non-finite coordinates crashed a clip boolean (NaN in the kernel's spatial index). Booleans now refuse non-finite loops (D30). Found by fuzzing. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
| DEF-10 | fixed | geometry | Compositing was quadratic in loops stacked in one place (a step-and-repeat with zero step flashed every pad hundreds of times): 11 s for a 1 KB Gerber. Exact duplicates are now merged with a count (D30). Found by fuzzing. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
| DEF-9 | fixed | readers | Small files could expand without bound: block arrays of block arrays (DXF), step-and-repeat counts (Gerber), recursive references (GDSII), forms invoking forms or themselves (PDF), dictionary primitives placed by many pads (IPC-2581). Now bounded by the import work budget (D31); self-invoking forms are skipped. Found by fuzzing. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
| DEF-8 | fixed | geometry | Compositing hung (over 10 minutes, 2.5 GB) when one loop had extreme coordinates: the bounding-box grid visited every cell between them. The grid is now bounded, and primitives beyond 1 km from the origin or with non-finite values are dropped at import with an error. Found by fuzzing. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
| DEF-7 | fixed | gerber | A non-ASCII byte after `%` panicked the reader (string split inside a multibyte character). Found by fuzzing. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
| DEF-6 | fixed | geometry | The union of two pads a few nanometres apart (duplicate flashes with rounding differences) could be empty: our join tolerance was finer than the kernel's, which reports such near-tangent crossings inconsistently (D30). Found by fuzzing. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
| DEF-5 | fixed | geometry | Self-intersecting loops were not split at their own crossings: a bow-tie fill came out empty under both fill rules and a self-intersecting quad kept a negative lobe (D30). Affected SVG/PDF fills, Gerber regions and DXF hatches with self-crossing boundaries. Found by fuzzing. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
| DEF-4 | fixed | build | Debug wasm trapped (`unreachable`) in the standard library's `hypot` during bleed-aware planning: build-std compiled `compiler_builtins` with the dev profile's overflow checks, and its libm relies on wraparound. `just wasm` now disables overflow checks and debug assertions for `compiler_builtins`. Release builds (CI, Pages) were not affected. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |

The worker is now spawned on first use. Found by checking the published site. | 2026-10-25 | 2026-10-25 |
